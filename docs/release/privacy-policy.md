# Privacy Policy — Gym Buddies

_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

Gym Buddies does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server. We operate no server, and there is no account to create. The app does not use your location: "open now" for a gym comes from the hours you entered and the phone's clock.

## What the app stores
Your exercises, workouts, logged sets, notes, gyms (the names, equipment, hours, prices and travel times you type) and settings are kept in one database file on your device, with automatic backup copies of it in the app's own storage on the device: up to 20 from today and one from each of the 6 days before. They stay there unless you turn on iCloud backup or export them.

## What leaves your device, and only if you ask
- **iCloud backup** — off by default. When you turn it on, a copy of the database is saved to your own iCloud Drive (iCloud Drive → Gym Buddies), one file per day kept for 30 days, in your Apple account and your storage quota. We have no access to it.
- **Backup export** — the database file, handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email) is your choice and under your account with that service.
- **CSV export** — your sessions as a spreadsheet file, the same way.
- **Workout export** — a plain-text list of a workout's exercises, the same way.

Apart from iCloud Drive, which iOS syncs on your behalf, the app makes no network requests at all.

## Notifications
The rest timer and timed sets schedule local notifications on your device. They are created and delivered by iOS on the phone; nothing is sent anywhere. Turn them off in Settings → Rest.

## Analytics and advertising
None. No analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.

## Health information
Gym Buddies records the sets you log — reps, weights, times, laps — and nothing else about your body or health. Workout templates, including the pregnancy ones, are general exercise starting points, not medical advice. It does not read or write Apple Health.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Deleting your data
Deleting the app removes its database and its on-device backups. iCloud backups and files you exported remain yours to delete — in the Files app, or in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.
